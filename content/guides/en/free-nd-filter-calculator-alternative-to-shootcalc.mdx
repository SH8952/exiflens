If you've searched for an ND filter long-exposure calculator, you may have come across ShootCalc.com — a small, free, web-based calculator with forward and reverse exposure modes, filter stacking support, and a handful of related calculators. ExifLens has its own free ND filter calculator built into the homepage, and it's worth comparing the two honestly rather than assuming one is simply "better."

## Disclosure

This article mentions ShootCalc, an independent website unrelated to ExifLens. ExifLens is not affiliated with or endorsed by ShootCalc. Feature details referenced here reflect ShootCalc's public site as of the time this article was written (September 2026) and may have changed since — check shootcalc.com directly for its current feature set.

## What both calculators share

Both ExifLens and ShootCalc offer forward mode (enter your base exposure and ND filter strength, get the resulting long-exposure shutter speed) and reverse mode (enter your target shutter speed, get the filter strength needed), along with support for stacking multiple ND filters. Functionally, the core ND math is the same regardless of which site you use — it's standard exposure-value arithmetic, not something either site has an exclusive claim to.

## Where the two differ

ExifLens's ND filter calculator sits inside a larger site with related calculators (exposure, DoF, timelapse, astro) and guide articles covering ND filter types, filter stacking pitfalls, and graduated ND use, all available in four languages (English, Korean, Japanese, Spanish). ShootCalc's related-calculator set includes its own DoF, crop factor, and shutter-count-checker tools — a shutter count checker being something ExifLens doesn't currently offer (it's on ExifLens's own roadmap, but isn't live yet).

## Which one to use

If you specifically need a shutter count checker today, ShootCalc's related toolset covers that in a way ExifLens doesn't yet. For the core ND filter exposure math itself, and if you'd like it alongside a broader library of camera-setting guides and calculators in your own language, ExifLens's version covers the same ground for free.

## Checklist

- Both calculators support forward/reverse ND exposure modes and filter stacking — the underlying math is standard and not unique to either site.
- ExifLens pairs its ND calculator with a wider library of related calculators and guides in four languages.
- ShootCalc currently offers a shutter count checker that ExifLens doesn't yet have live.
- Either is a legitimate free option — pick based on which related tools you actually need alongside the ND calculation.
