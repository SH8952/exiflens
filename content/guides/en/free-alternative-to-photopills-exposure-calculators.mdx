PhotoPills is one of the most established names in photography planning apps, known for its depth-of-field and exposure calculators, and especially its sun/moon/Milky Way planning tools with augmented-reality overlays. It's a paid, one-time-purchase mobile app for iOS and Android. If what you're looking for is specifically a free way to run core exposure and depth-of-field calculations in a browser — without installing an app or making a purchase — here's an honest look at where ExifLens overlaps with PhotoPills, and where it doesn't.

## Where the two overlap

ExifLens offers free, web-based calculators covering several of the same core categories PhotoPills is known for: depth of field, the exposure triangle, hyperfocal distance, bracketing/HDR exposure steps, astrophotography exposure via the NPF rule, and timelapse interval/storage planning. Because these run directly in a browser, there's nothing to install and no purchase required — useful if you want a quick calculation on a desktop, or on a phone you're not willing to install a paid app on.

## Where PhotoPills goes further

To be upfront about it: PhotoPills covers considerably more ground than a free web tool can reasonably match. Its augmented-reality sun, moon, and Milky Way direction planning, offline map-based shoot planning, and its own deeper feature layers (like Advanced DoF and Macro DoF) are built for a dedicated planning app and aren't something ExifLens's web calculators attempt to replace. If you regularly plan landscape or astro shoots around exact sun/moon positions at a specific location, PhotoPills' app-based approach is doing a job a browser calculator isn't designed for.

## When the free web route makes sense

If your actual need is running a quick depth-of-field, exposure, or NPF-rule calculation — at your desk, without downloading anything, in whatever language you're comfortable in — ExifLens's calculators cover that without a purchase. It's not a wholesale replacement for PhotoPills' planning suite; it's a free option for the calculator-only part of that need.

## Checklist

- PhotoPills is a paid mobile app; ExifLens's calculators are free and run in any browser.
- Core overlap: depth of field, exposure triangle, hyperfocal distance, bracketing, astro (NPF rule), timelapse.
- PhotoPills' AR planning, map-based shoot planning, and advanced macro/DoF layers go beyond what a browser calculator does.
- Choose based on what you actually need: a quick free calculation, or a full offline planning app.
